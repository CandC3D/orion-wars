    HOW A TURN WORKS

    1. Impacts. Missiles launched last turn arrive now: point defence may intercept them, small hulls may evade, the rest hit.
    2. Power. Every ship's power pool refills. A spinal capacitor draws its charge. Your shield reserve is held back for absorption; the rest is for movement and guns.
    3. Three action rounds. Each round, initiative is rolled and every ship takes one action: move as planned (turn up to its turn rate, then run forward along its facing), or hold and fire every weapon that bears on a target in range. A carrier's strike wave goes in during round 1. Wrecks detonate at the end of each round.
    4. End. The turn closes; missiles fired this turn are in flight until next turn's impacts.

    Select an action to inspect its predicted position, heading and weapon allocations. The forecast assumes stationary contacts and no damage: eligibility is not a guaranteed shot. A blocked move or a turn-only order still consumes its action. Mounts normally fire once per turn.

    Q/E turn port/starboard; W/S adjust distance; H holds; 1–3 select the action. Enter commits from the plot/page, never behind a dialog. Tab moves through controls normally. A preferred target allows fallback to other legal targets.

    The scripted opponent lives under exactly the same rules: one action per ship per round.
