# Review gate - revision 05, written before implementation, 10 September 2026

Every criterion and every hull's metal assessment must independently reach 90/100.
The weighted total cannot rescue a failed criterion. Inspect native planning frames
before detail views. Visual scores require actually opening the captured images.
Revision 04 art scores are withdrawn: Earth's primary metal was wrongly classified.

| Criterion | Weight | Adversarial evidence and deductions |
|---|---:|---|
| Brief and scope fidelity | 10% | Only this prototype and its three current frigates. Furniture unchanged, black stands, no identifier decision, no new samples, intake, shared runtime, branch or commit. |
| Fidelity to generated art and region classification | 25% | Re-derive every source triangle's colour and area; verify reduced coverage and exact metal masks. Earth steel, Krelath bronze, Vraygon gold are substantial materials. Every region has a classification, evidence and uncertainty. No palette swapping, global colour semantics or invented anatomy. |
| Metal reads as period metallic paint, never chrome | 25% | Score each hull separately at planning and close range: substantial steel/bronze/gold areas, common rough flake-binder finish, dark washed recesses and brighter burnished raised edges. Deduct for white paint, flat brown/yellow, polished mirrors, broad plastic highlights or unstable glitter. |
| Painted body and period/register fidelity | 15% | Matte opaque block coats, readable real-edge lining/drybrush, sparse actual-corner wear. No modern finish or invented detail. All source regions physically lit and non-emissive. Explicit per-hull, per-region energy map; no auto-glow from designation and no guessed weapons. |
| Scale coherence | 10% | Existing 10 mm/unit table remains accurate and unchanged; 55/65/45 mm frigates with black uniform 30 mm posts. No credit for the untested cross-class ladder. |
| Unaided play-element reading | 15% | All three read as painted miniatures at the native planning camera before close-up inspection. Steel with blue panels, green with bronze, and gold with violet/yellow remain source-faithful. Camera height/pitch floors enforced. |

100 means no defect found within this slice. Record deductions, per-hull metal
scores, final frame hashes and measurable evidence. Iterate privately on anything
under 90. If blocked, report the actual score and blocker; never substitute a
geometry subscore for visual review. All material and art scores are fresh.

Out of scope and unscored: full-board identification/performance, replacement
identifier decision, cross-class scale ladder, destruction, planets/nebulae,
permanent intake and unmeasured GPU memory. Source questions remain explicit:
Krelath green distinction/smaller dome; Shard yellow function; any other region
whose function or metallic intent lacks authority. Reproduce uncertain colours
faithfully without manufacturing a system designation.
