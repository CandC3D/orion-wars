# Review gate — written before revision work, 10 September 2026

Every criterion must score at least 90/100. The weighted total cannot conceal a
failed criterion. A missing visual inspection is **unscored**, never a pass.
The reviewer can see images through the image viewer and must open the final
captures, including close views, before assigning visual scores.

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence required / reasons to deduct |
|---|---:|---|
| Brief and scope fidelity | 20% | Correct local derivative, three hulls, one developed paint candidate, black bases/posts, unchanged shared runtime, existing playback clock and information boundary. Any contrary behaviour is a failure. |
| Fidelity to generated art | 25% | Compare the current Sparrowhawk GLB, its material regions and v2 schematic with the derivative and captured hull. Preserve silhouette, domes, nacelle, collector, stern functions and authored warnings; no invented panels or guessed trim. Visible feature loss or misplaced paint fails. |
| Period materials and painting | 20% | Inspect the actual frame and detail capture: cared-for block painting, real recess lining, restrained raised-edge work; opaque physical objects, printed card, black painted stands. Generic spatial paint bands, modern gradients, glowing physical paint or decorative surface density fail. |
| Scale coherence | 20% | One declared conversion; generated dimension table checked against scene geometry and the supplied real sizes. Inspect the frame for implausible relative size. Unmeasured main objects or an incorrect mug/die/book fail. |
| Unaided tabletop reading | 15% | View the finished frame without relying on labels: weight, miniature scale, paper edge, domestic table, restrained effects and permitted camera angles. Inspect planning and resolution. Full-board identification is expressly untested and cannot be scored by this slice. |

Scoring: 100 means no defect found within the stated slice; deduct explicitly
for each observed limitation. A known unmet requirement cannot be counted as
an aesthetic preference. Automated checks support measurable claims; they do
not establish visual quality. Record final evidence, individual deductions and
remaining unscored work in REVIEW.md. Intermediate failed frames and scores stay
local; do not submit them as accepted work. If the gate cannot be met, report
the failed criterion, its honest score and the concrete blocker.

Out of scope and unscored: destruction, planet/nebula samples, full-board
performance/readability, permanent asset intake, and Chris's choice of a new
identification system. Blank black bases do not imply that choice has been made.
