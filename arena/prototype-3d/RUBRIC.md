# Review gate - revision 04, written before implementation, 10 September 2026

Every criterion must independently score at least 90/100. A weighted total cannot
rescue a failed criterion. Visual criteria require directly opening the final
captured images; code and geometry measurements are supporting evidence only.

This round reviews three current frigates and their paint at the actual planning
camera first. Macro captures verify placement afterward. Chris approved the
Sparrowhawk art placement; the surface treatment still needs improvement on all
three. Furniture is an unchanged placeholder and is not an improvement target.

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence required / reasons to deduct |
|---|---:|---|
| Brief and scope fidelity | 15% | Three current source frigates and local derivatives, all developed to the same treatment. Black stands, shared clock, camera floors and projection retained. No furniture, shared runtime, asset intake or other-session changes. |
| Fidelity to generated art | 25% | Per-hull COLOR_0 regions, source counts, geometry and permitted references. Monoceros v3 schematic and Earth conventions; Sparrowhawk v2; Shard model alone. No global colour-to-function mapping, invented anatomy or guessed fixtures. Compare original and reduced surfaces and inspect all hulls. |
| Reads as painted metal rather than rendered plastic | 25% | At unzoomed planning height, matte body, visible real recess lining and drybrush; at close range, uneven opaque coverage, restrained edge wear and occasional corner chips. Bronze/metallic paint must be dull with local sparkle, never broad chrome highlights. Gloss confined to ink pools and picked metallics. Any hull failing this is a criterion failure, not averaged with its neighbours. |
| Period technique and register fidelity | 10% | Black undercoat, block coats, ink pools, chalky drybrush and hand-picked edges on cast metal. No modern gradients, invented panels or added detail density. Bright paint remains physical and non-emissive; imagined energy is separate at confirmed features. |
| Scale coherence | 10% | Republish the measured scale table using 10 mm/unit; all existing furniture dimensions unchanged. Current frigates remain 55/65/45 mm with uniform 30 mm posts seated on their actual undersides. Cross-class ladder receives no credit. |
| Unaided play-element reading | 15% | Three distinguishable painted miniatures on black stands, clear at planning and resolution, without relying on captions or forbidden low angles. Inspect the ordinary board image at native size before close-ups. Full-board identification remains untested. |

100 means no defect found within this slice. Record explicit deductions and
separate per-hull material assessments. All three material assessments must also
reach 90. Adversarial review must seek misplaced colour, smooth plastic flanks,
chrome metallics, invisible arm's-length brushwork, invented wear on flat panels,
lost small fixtures, false muzzle/glow locations and scale errors.

Intermediate failed frames and scores stay local. Submit only the final passing
frames and their hashes. If a criterion cannot pass, report its score and concrete
blocker rather than silently omitting it or substituting a geometry subscore.

Unscored outside scope: replacement identification choice and full-board
readability/performance, cross-class scale ladder, destruction, planet/nebula
samples, permanent intake, and deferred target-machine/memory measurements.
Sparrowhawk's two greens and smaller dome remain unanswered fiction; reproduce
their actual appearance without assigning meanings.
