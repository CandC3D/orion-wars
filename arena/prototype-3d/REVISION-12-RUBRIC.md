# Earth and Krelath window normals — rubric before implementation

Every criterion must reach 90/100 independently. Scores will be derived again
from this round's evidence, not inherited from the prior sheets. Native-resolution
images will be opened and inspected directly; I can see those images. Numerical
normal and topology checks support, but cannot replace, the visual judgement.

| Per-hull criterion | Weight | Gate |
|---|---:|---|
| No pinching at any window cluster, and no new faceting on curved surfaces | 40% | Inspect recessed detail and curved forms from both sides and above; smooth command spheres, cylinders and organic surfaces; no triangular shading fans. |
| Source geometry and material-region fidelity | 25% | No source edits; unchanged prepared surface positions, triangle connectivity/colours, scale and small components; palette and attachment validation. |
| Approved miniature treatment | 20% | Period controlled lining, bright steel/bronze, inert bright paint, clear posts, black FASA base, supplied codes and approved fighter formation preserved. |
| Inspection readability and completeness | 15% | Same quarter/side inspection cameras, no clipping, readable rim code; matched before/after evidence for every changed hull. |

| Per-sheet criterion | Weight | Gate |
|---|---:|---|
| Complete approved inventory | 25% | All seven Earth and eleven active Krelath entries, including both six-fighter flights and Intrallus; retired Boss absent. |
| Corrected windows and retained curvature | 35% | Every hull passes its own normal-quality gate; no failed hull hidden by sheet downscaling. |
| Comparison, captions and approved scale | 25% | Unchanged inspection views and shared-scale strip; names, types, codes, lengths and approved ladder accurate. |
| Layout and evidence integrity | 15% | No clipping/overflow; captures and score hashes correspond to the corrected derivatives. |

Diagnosis precedes correction: at least Acamar and one Krelath hull will be shown
with identical geometry, camera and neutral material, changing only normals.
Candidate crease thresholds will be compared before choosing one. All active
Earth/Krelath hulls will be checked, including small recessed features on fighters.
The Vraygon GLBs, maps, images and sheet will be fingerprinted and left untouched.
The pending pre-alpha reel is not part of this pass.
