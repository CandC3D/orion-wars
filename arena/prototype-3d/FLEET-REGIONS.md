# Complete-library region tables

Generated by `node arena/prototype-3d/build-fleet-sheets.mjs` from source triangle areas and the faction contract. Surface share includes all exported triangle faces, including interior/overlapping surfaces; it is not camera-visible area or vertex count. Every region is included; shares are rounded to three decimals and exact values remain in inventory.json. All material classes emit zero in the physical register. Green Vraygon is **moulded transparent** by default. The other colours retain their faction-specific classifications.

Every emissive-designated region has its exact prepared face membership in `prepared/fleet/<id>-regions.json → energyAttachments`. The smaller dorsal domes on Sparrowhawk, Swift, Ballista and Raptor are now mapped as point-defence emitters using Chris’s ruling and verified matching geometry; designation is not activation. Every source region classifies under Chris’s faction contract. Material meaning is not a licence to label unknown anatomy.
