# Board lettering — rubric before implementation

Direct inspection of the supplied shapes and captured board is required. Every
scored criterion must reach 90/100; passing code checks cannot substitute for
reading the printed result. No earlier material score is carried forward.

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence required |
|---|---:|---|
| Supplied art fidelity | 30% | All outlined paths, proportions, registered mark and corrected Achernar hook retained; cream originals unchanged. |
| Printed-component treatment | 25% | Board dark spot ink on cream card; restrained registration and tint texture consistent with the board; no floating overlay, gloss or emission. |
| Header composition and camera reading | 25% | Both titles distinguishable, stock-type SECTOR SHEET 01 retained, no collision or clipping; angular cuts hold in resolution view. Check planning too, and report any limit. |
| Scope and rendering cost | 20% | Only prototype board branding changes; furniture untouched, camera unchanged, existing budgets and browser checks pass; source-to-render regeneration documented. |

Unscored: native planning readability of the small registered symbol as separate
text, brand treatment of the deferred box lid, full-board class-code reading and
the postponed scale/performance test. The registered symbol must still be present
in the artwork and resolvable in the board-header inspection capture.
