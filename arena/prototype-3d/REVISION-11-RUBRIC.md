# Fleet corrections — rubric before implementation

This pass starts at 5dd04ba and follows Chris's rulings through 0bcc8e9.
Inspect real captures directly. Do not carry forward the earlier hull scores.
Every scored criterion must reach 90; failed readability measurements must still
be reported, and cannot be averaged away inside a successful material score.

## Each of the 25 retained hulls / flight units

| Criterion | Weight | Required evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Generated-art fidelity and diagnosed geometry | 25% | Exact faction regions and source geometry; diagnose window pinching before fixes, compare two Vraygon hulls in matched views; map only positively identified point-defence domes. |
| Practised-painter lining | 25% | Consistent physical line width, continuous recess lines, controlled coverage across like features and all factions; no arbitrary panel art or scratchy breaks. |
| Period physical materials | 25% | Bright silver-white Earth steel, original blues, dull flake metals; clear green Vraygon weapons and clear posts, no paint strokes on plastic or physical emission. |
| Approved mounting, codes and scale | 15% | Black FASA base, transparent tapered supports, codes on arcs 2/4/6, cast facing triangle; fighters six per flight; approved compressed ladder. |
| Inspection readability and composition | 10% | Unchanged inspection orientations, matched side/quarter views, readable supplied codes, complete unclipped assembly. |

## Board, assessed separately

| Criterion | Weight | Required evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Planning comprehension | 25% | High near-orthographic deep-focus camera; complete board, projected size/perspective measurements. |
| Resolution move and macro character | 20% | Deliberate descent and lens change on playback clock; shallow focus and hard floors through the complete move. |
| Approved physical treatment | 20% | Fleet materials, clear supports, face-correct codes and triangle; all six headings verified against hex.js. |
| Cast-triangle legibility at planning height | 20% | Native pixels, contrast and direct visual judgement, at all six headings. Subtle casting is not permission to claim an invisible triangle works. |
| Print margin, budgets and regressions | 15% | Grid clipped below unchanged header art; measured clear-default costs, appropriate tests, scope contained. |

Rim-code legibility at the new planning camera is a **separate measured score**,
never masked by the board total. Report a failure even when inspection sheets
pass. Contact-sheet cameras are not substitutes for game-camera evidence.
Full-board occupancy/performance, target-device coverage, destruction, planets
and nebulae remain outside this pass and unscored. Source-art faults that cannot
be corrected here are documented and withheld rather than cosmetically hidden.
