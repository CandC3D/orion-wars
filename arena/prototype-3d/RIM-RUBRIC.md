# Rim-code gate — revision 08, written before implementation

Each scored criterion must reach 90 independently. A failed legibility test is a
finding to report, not a reason to enlarge the skirt, move lettering to the roof,
lower the camera, add an overlay or invent an Earth code. Failed candidates remain
in the isolated measurement study and are not enabled on the accepted board.

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence and failure conditions |
|---|---:|---|
| Rim-code legibility at planning height | 40% | Directly read both complete supplied codes in native, unscaled planning captures. Measure physical cap height, projected cap height/width and fit on the existing 3 mm skirt. Test a size ladder up to the actual geometric limit. A count of pixels alone cannot pass reading. No minimum is claimed if none fits and reads. |
| Source fidelity | 15% | KFG-01 and VFG-04 exactly; no Earth designation. No faction abbreviation added, no facing symbol, no inferred larger-hull correction. |
| Physical placement and orientation | 15% | Entire label remains on the bottom bevelled skirt, below its top edge. Six repeated codes, one centred per face, avoid an authored front and provide a camera-facing face at every heading. Check all six game headings and between-heading transit; retain true depth/occlusion. |
| Period printing and visual restraint | 15% | Period typeface and a declared decal/paper process, lit nonemitting ink. No modern UI label, billboard, emissive text or thickened base. Examine close-up as well as planning. |
| Scope, repeatability and scale | 15% | Black opaque posts, unchanged furniture/hulls/cameras/base shape and dimensions; no shared runtime changes, branch or commit. Deterministic captures and relevant tests, measured study limits. |

Earth class-code legibility is explicitly **unscored — designation pending Chris**.
Its rim must be visibly blank. No weighted total may disguise this pending item or
a failed Krelath/Vraygon reading score. Facing and full-board readability remain
open and unscored; neither is designed or tested in this slice.

Use the current 1600 × 1080 browser review at device scale 1 and the renderer's
existing pixel cap. Native captures decide; enlarged crops only show why a size
fails. A six-heading sweep is a local three-piece orientation check, not a
full-board scale test.
