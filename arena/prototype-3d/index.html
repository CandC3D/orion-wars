01 / THE PAINTED FLEETS

One brush standard.

Monoceros, Sparrowhawk and Shard carry their own authored colours. Matte coats, dark recesses, chalky raised edges and dull metallic paint belong to the miniatures. Bright paint emits nothing; imagined energy remains a separate layer.

02 / THE CAMERA & LIGHT

The room stays in frame.

A warm key casts the room’s shadows. Clear posts are uniformly 30 mm high. Planning is high, near-orthographic and deep-focus; resolution descends and changes lens over 1200 ms, staying above 24 units and a 32° downward angle. Camera and effects share the existing playback clock.

03 / PROVISIONAL BUDGETS

Bound the first frame.

One 2048² shadow map. One transient event at a time; two beam layers. Half-resolution bloom at 0.18 gain. Resolution blur ≤4 px. Render buffer ≤2.07M pixels; submitted triangles ≤110k and draw calls ≤128, including shadows and post-processing.

04 / WHAT NEEDS MEASURING

This is the small gate.

Still to measure: target-GPU frame time, mobile and integrated-GPU headroom, ink and base readability at full-board scale. Nebula proposal: ≤0.06 linear contribution, ≤15% board coverage and ≤0.012 mean. Destruction and the 72×40 scale test await this frame’s review.
