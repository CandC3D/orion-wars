01 / THE PAINT CANDIDATE

One brush standard.

The Earth frigate carries the candidate: opaque blue enamel over black, pooling ink lines, chalky drybrush marks and picked silver collars. Krelath and Vraygon are basecoat references. Original meshes and exports are untouched.

02 / THE CAMERA & LIGHT

The room stays in frame.

A warm key casts the room’s shadows. Uniform posts are 1.6 units high. Planning is deep-focus; resolution descends over 900 ms, staying above 12 units and a 32° downward angle. Camera and effects share the existing playback clock.

03 / PROVISIONAL BUDGETS

Bound the first frame.

One 2048² shadow map. One transient event at a time; two beam layers. Half-resolution bloom at 0.18 gain. Resolution blur ≤3 px. Render buffer ≤2.07M pixels; submitted triangles ≤70k and draw calls ≤90, including shadows and post-processing.

04 / WHAT NEEDS MEASURING

This is the small gate.

Still to measure: target-GPU frame time, mobile and integrated-GPU headroom, ink and base readability at full-board scale. Nebula proposal: ≤0.06 linear contribution, ≤15% board coverage and ≤0.012 mean. Destruction and the 72×40 scale test await this frame’s review.
