# Complete library — provisional miniature ladder

1 scene unit = 10 mm. The contact-sheet ladder is a proposal, not rules scale or an approved class-size contract. The existing three-frigate board retains its earlier 55 / 65 / 45 mm sizes; only its class-code entries change.

Calibration: Monoceros provenance supplies 205 m at 93.42599868774414 source units. The same source-unit factor agrees with the supplied Earth class plates and Krelath Ballista/Sparrowhawk plates. Vraygon has no independent physical-length calibration in the staged references: applying the same factor there is explicitly provisional. Every source has a derivable longitudinal bounding length; beam is not substituted for length, including the swept-wing Bomber.

Proposed compression: **55 × sqrt(calibrated length / 205 m) mm**. One formula across factions/classes preserves model ordering while giving the smallest fighter a 9.45 mm piece and the flagship a 96.90 mm piece. It deliberately does not force a type hierarchy onto the source: Swift, Sparrowhawk and Ballista share a source length, and Avalanche is shorter than Shard. Native longitudinal axes are explicit in fleet-assets.json. This presentation orientation adds no facing mark or rules authority.

The pyramid rise and exposed post length are independent. All objects remain on the same table/board geometry under the same room key. Furniture dimensions remain as published in SCALE.md. Large-hull stability and full-board occupancy remain untested; this is an inspection set, not the postponed full-board test.

The strip uses the same 1000 × 720 orthographic camera, 300 mm horizontal span, for all 26 source captures. Composition applies one common image factor 1.00000000 and only translates/crops. Final strip scale: 3.33333333 px/mm. All three sheets have the same scale and vertical crop. Individual view pairs fit the full assembly and are labelled accordingly.
