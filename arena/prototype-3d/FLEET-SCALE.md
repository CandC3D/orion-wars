# Complete library — approved miniature ladder

1 scene unit = 10 mm. Chris approved the compressed presentation ladder on 10 September 2026. The board now matches it: Earth 55.00 mm, Krelath 54.33 mm, Vraygon 50.40 mm. This is not rules scale.

Calibration: Monoceros provenance supplies 205 m at 93.42599868774414 source units. The same source-unit factor agrees with the supplied Earth class plates and Krelath Ballista/Sparrowhawk plates. Vraygon has no independent physical-length calibration in the staged references: the approved ladder applies the same factor there, without inventing independent fictional-length evidence. Every source has a derivable longitudinal bounding length; beam is not substituted for length, including the swept-wing Bomber.

Approved compression: **55 × sqrt(calibrated length / 205 m) mm**. One formula across factions/classes preserves model ordering while giving the smallest fighter a 9.45 mm piece and the flagship a 96.90 mm piece. It deliberately does not force a type hierarchy onto the source: Swift, Sparrowhawk and Ballista share a source length, and Avalanche is shorter than Shard. Native longitudinal axes are explicit in fleet-assets.json. Facing is now the cast arc-2 triangle; physical scale adds no rules authority.
